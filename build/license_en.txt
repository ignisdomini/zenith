ZENIT — zenith projections of celestial bodies
Version 1.2.0
Developer: Apostol A. V.
Phone: +7 925 888-08-66
E-mail: apostolgroup@gmail.com

The program shows on a map the points where stars, the Sun, the Moon and the planets are at the zenith, computes zenith passages through a ground area and searches for anomalous zenith intersection points.

Accuracy. Apparent places of stars agree with the astronomy-engine reference to 0.01″. The deflection of the vertical (up to 30″ in mountains), polar motion (about 10 m) and DUT1 (unless set in the settings) are not taken into account. Planet positions are accurate to about 1′. Results are for reference only.
